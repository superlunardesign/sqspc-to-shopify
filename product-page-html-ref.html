If your normal to dry skin feels easily overwhelmed, reactive, or like it just needs a break, this toner was made for you.

Soothing Radiance Toner is a gentle, calming step designed for skin that flushes, tightens, stings, or never seems fully comfortable.

It brings the skin back to neutral after cleansing, easing irritation while quietly supporting hydration and balance so the rest of your routine can actually do its job.

This is not a stripping toner. It does not tingle, burn, or dry you out. Instead, it uses soothing botanicals like licorice root, centella, green tea, and cucumber to calm visible redness, support the skin barrier, and leave skin feeling soft, steady, and grounded.

Perfect for sensitive, acne-prone, or aging skin, this toner is often the missing step for clients who feel like their skin is always on edge.
